# G-Unit Cakes Brand Identity

Version 1.0 - July 2026

## Brand Core

### Brand essence

Celebration, made personal.

### Brand promise

Every G-Unit Cakes order should feel thoughtfully designed, carefully made, clearly communicated, and joyful to receive.

### Mission

To bake memorable cakes and sweet details that make every celebration feel personal.

### Vision

To become a trusted local cake studio known for thoughtful design, generous flavour, and warm service.

### Positioning

G-Unit Cakes is a custom cake studio for people who want a celebration piece that looks considered, tastes generous, and feels made specifically for their moment.

### Personality

- Elegant, not formal
- Warm, not overly familiar
- Creative, not chaotic
- Confident, not boastful
- Reliable, not transactional

### Recommended tagline

Made for your moment.

## Audience

### Primary

People ordering birthday, celebration, wedding, baby shower, graduation, anniversary, and milestone cakes.

### Secondary

Gift buyers, event planners, small businesses, and customers ordering cupcakes, cookies, dessert boxes, or branded treats.

## Messaging

### Primary message

Beautifully considered cakes, baked for the moments worth celebrating.

### Supporting messages

- Personal by design: each order begins with the customer's occasion, taste, and visual references.
- Beautiful and delicious: the finish should create a strong first impression without compromising flavour.
- Clear from enquiry to collection: ordering should feel welcoming, organised, and dependable.
- Thoughtful in every detail: decoration, finishing, packaging, and handover should feel connected.

### Calls to action

- Start your custom order
- Tell us about your celebration
- Explore recent cakes
- Check availability
- Order on WhatsApp

## Voice

### Voice traits

- Warm: speak with genuine care and hospitality.
- Celebratory: acknowledge the meaning of the occasion.
- Clear: make dates, pricing, portions, and next steps easy to understand.
- Confident: guide customers with calm expertise.
- Personal: sound like a real baker speaking to a real customer.

### Writing rules

- Use short, natural sentences.
- Lead with the customer's occasion or desired outcome.
- Be specific about what is included and what happens next.
- Use one or two expressive details instead of excessive exclamation marks.
- Avoid jargon, vague luxury claims, and pressure-based selling.

### Sample caption

Soft coral piping, hand-finished details, and a centre made for celebrating. Planning something special? Tell us the date, servings, flavours, and the look you love.

### Sample enquiry reply

Thank you for thinking of G-Unit Cakes for your celebration. Please send your event date, number of servings, preferred flavours, and any inspiration images. We will confirm availability and guide you through the next step.

## Logo System

### Primary logo

The primary mark combines a whisk-shaped letter G, a coral frosting flourish, a black serif wordmark, a gold handle, and a small heart flourish.

### Meaning

- Whisk: craft, movement, and handmade baking.
- Letter G: immediate recognition and ownership of the name.
- Frosting flourish: celebration, decoration, and expressive cake design.
- Heart detail: care and personal service.
- Gold handle: warmth, quality, and a crafted finish.

### Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the word CAKES. No text, border, image, or package edge should enter this zone.

### Minimum size

- Digital full logo: 140 px wide minimum
- Digital symbol: 40 px wide minimum
- Print full logo: 38 mm wide minimum
- Print symbol: 12 mm wide minimum

### Approved use

- Full colour on white, ivory, blush, or pale neutral backgrounds
- Dark monochrome on white or ivory when colour is unavailable
- White/reversed version only on solid charcoal or photographic backgrounds with high contrast

### Do not

- Stretch, compress, rotate, crop, or rearrange the logo
- Change individual logo colours
- Add shadows, bevels, glows, outlines, or gradients
- Place it over busy cake photography without a quiet field
- Use the detailed full logo below the minimum size

### Production note

The screenshot-matched SVG preserves the supplied artwork by embedding cleaned raster artwork. It is suitable for standard digital use. Before signage, embroidery, foil stamping, laser cutting, or large-format print, create and approve a fully path-traced vector master.

## Typography

### Preferred brand fonts

- Display and headings: Cormorant Garamond, Semibold or Medium
- Body and interface: Manrope, Regular or Medium

### Reliable fallbacks

- Display fallback: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif
- Body fallback: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif

### Usage

- Keep display typography elegant and spacious.
- Use sentence case for headings and calls to action.
- Reserve all caps for short labels only.
- Use body text at 16 px minimum for digital applications.
- Do not use more than two font families in one design.

## Visual Language

### Photography

- Use bright, natural light with accurate frosting and cake colours.
- Show the real cake clearly before using atmospheric details.
- Mix complete cake portraits with close details of piping, texture, slices, and hands at work.
- Prefer clean ivory, blush, stone, pale wood, or charcoal surfaces.
- Keep edits warm, soft, and true to the product.

### Composition

- Leave quiet space for copy.
- Use close crops deliberately, never accidentally clipping the cake.
- Keep props limited and relevant to the flavour or occasion.
- Use straight-on or gentle three-quarter views for hero images.

### Graphic devices

- Fine coral rules and flourishes
- Small heart detail used sparingly
- Circular seals for stickers and social avatars
- Ivory fields with charcoal typography
- Coral or blush bands for package sleeves and post templates

## Packaging

### Core system

- White rigid cake box with full primary logo
- Blush handled box with circular seal
- Kraft cupcake box with coral belly band
- Ivory thank-you card with fine coral border
- Ivory sticker sheet with full-logo and symbol variants
- Clear treat bag finished with coral ribbon and logo seal

### Packaging principles

- Product protection comes first.
- The logo should be visible at handover and in customer photographs.
- Keep materials clean, food-safe, and easy to dispose of or recycle.
- Use no more than one large logo and one small seal on a single package face.
- Maintain accurate coral colour across paper, ribbon, labels, and digital previews.

## Digital and Social

### Website

- Use the full logo in the header and a simplified symbol for the favicon.
- Lead with real cake photography and a direct custom-order action.
- Keep ordering fields concise: event date, servings, flavour, style, budget, delivery or collection, and inspiration images.
- Instagram, WhatsApp, and TikTok links remain placeholders until the owner supplies the final accounts.

### Instagram and TikTok

- Use 4:5 portrait for feed posts and 9:16 for stories and short video.
- Alternate finished cakes, detail shots, process, customer moments, and ordering prompts.
- Keep text overlays short and within safe margins.
- Use the circular G/whisk seal as the profile image once a clean symbol-only asset is approved.

### WhatsApp

- Use clear, friendly automated greetings.
- Confirm availability before discussing detailed decoration.
- Summarise the agreed design, servings, flavour, price, payment status, and collection or delivery details in one message.
